Seed To Spoon

How Kanha Ji Oils Are Crafted

A transparent 6-step journey maintaining purity, nutrition, and natural flavor.

01

Seed Selection

Quality-focused sourcing of mature mustard, sesame, and groundnut seeds free from dampness or chemical residues.

02

Cleaning & Sorting

Multi-stage sieving removes husk and foreign dust particles, ensuring only 100% clean seeds enter the press.

03

Cold Pressing (Kachi Ghani)

Slow mechanical crushing without external heat keeping friction low, preserving essential nutrients and rich aroma.

04

Natural Filtration

Physical sedimentation and natural micro-cloth filtration. No chemical bleaching, acid washing, or artificial de-odorizers.

05

Purity & Quality Check

Each batch is monitored to guarantee 0% palm oil and zero synthetic additives before sealing.

06

Hygienic Packaging

Bottled in clean, tamper-evident containers ready for counter sales and fast doorstep dispatch in Indore.
