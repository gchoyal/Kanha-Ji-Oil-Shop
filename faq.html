Frequently Asked Questions

Factual, straightforward answers about Kanha Ji Oils, our cold-pressing techniques, packaging sizes, and ordering via WhatsApp.

We sell a comprehensive range of edible and traditional oils including 100% pure cold-pressed Mustard Oil (Kachi Ghani), Sesame (Til) Oil, Filtered Groundnut Oil, Soybean Oil, Coconut Oil, Pure Mahua Oil, Karanja Oil, and concentrated Neem Oil. In addition, we manufacture organic oil cakes (Neem khali, mustard cake, mahua cake) for agriculture, terrace gardening, and cattle feed.

Kachi Ghani refers to the traditional cold-pressing method where seeds are crushed slowly at low temperatures without external heat or chemical solvents. This preserves natural pungency, authentic aroma, and vital seed nutrients that are stripped away during conventional commercial refining.

Our oils are available in various bottle sizes to suit different household needs: 100ml (trial/pooja packs), 250ml, 500ml, and 1 Litre bottles, as well as 5 Litre jars and bulk containers for regular family kitchens or catering use. Oil cakes are available in 1kg, 5kg, 25kg, and 50kg bags. Please contact us on WhatsApp for current stock availability of specific sizes.
